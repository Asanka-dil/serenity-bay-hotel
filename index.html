    About US

    Where heritage meets elegance

    Welcome to Serenity Bay, where heritage meets elegance. Our hotel stands in a restored colonial-era villa, with arched verandas, tropical gardens and golden sunsets that make every evening special.

    Our spacious rooms, award-worthy restaurant and attentive staff come together to give you a stay that feels both luxurious and welcoming.

    At Serenity Bay, we don't just offer a room, we offer an experience rooted in true Sri Lankan hospitality.

    Serenity Bay is a heritage hotel offering comfort, great food and warm Sri Lankan hospitality. Relax in our peaceful gardens, enjoy fresh local cuisine and experience a stay you will never forget.
